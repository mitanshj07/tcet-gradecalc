# Disclaimer

TCET GradeCalc is an unofficial student tool. It is not affiliated with TCET.

The app estimates SGPA/CGPA using CBCGS-HME 2023-style grading rules and branch/semester/cycle subject templates. Official results, moderation, grace marks, rule changes, and institute notices always take priority.

The grading system and office-register concept are common across supported templates. The differences are subject layouts: course order, credits, TW/PR/OR components, and max marks.

Always verify important academic decisions against official TCET result documents and scheme notices.
